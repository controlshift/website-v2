Your supporters can't take action if they can't find the campaign. We've rolled out various updates to petition searches – making it easier for your supporters to connect with the campaigns that matter most to them. 

## Better search results

Better search means not only showing users petitions that match their keywords, but also accounting for the most active campaigns. To ensure that we're showing users the most currently relevant campaigns, we've released a new **activity score** metric. The activity score uses things like: how many signatures the petition has received in the last day, how recently the petition was updated, when the last blast email was sent to supporters, etc.

The new score means that people will automatically see current campaigns that are actively engaging supporters higher in their search results, rather than having to work through results that may be less relevant to their needs.

## Redesigned search page

We’ve also refreshed and redesigned the search results page. The new page is more modern includes **a new category filter and prompts** for users to take additional action. 

The new styling of the search results petition cards will also display categories information to make it easier for users to find the campaigns they're looking for. The most active petitions will also be tagged with a trending banner to highlight them further.

We've also added highlighting of matching text so that the user knows exactly why a petition is included in the search results. 

These improvements are designed to make the process more effective, ensuring that your supporters can find the campaigns they're looking for easily.

## Local campaigns now on the local organizing map

Location can be an important part of how people decide to get involved. Someone looking for a local issue or an opportunity to make a difference in their community needs a way to discover campaigns connected to the places that matter to them.

We’ve expanded the **local organizing map to include petitions with location information**, allowing users to see all of their local petitions, events, and groups in one place. Organizations will also have access to the various filters already available on that page (including filtering content by labels and regions) and while also being able to embed maps with petitions on external sites. 

By adding petitions to the local organizing map, we're providing another way for supporters to see the work your organization is doing and how it serves their local communities. 

## Helping more people take action

We're excited to announce these updates to searching on ControlShift. By making it easier to find campaigns, we're making it easier for supporters to get involved in your important work.
